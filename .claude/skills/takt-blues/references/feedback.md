## The brief

- **2026-10-24.** Takt is "the conservatory and the club": a groove is always playing, mastery
  is measured by MIDI, Level 1 is the blues. The band plays the moment the app opens.
- **2026-10-28, the Stage.** "a new game mode… in the style of Guitar Hero… two blues, two
  electronic… chords and melody lines… easy, medium, hard, or adaptive… no gate." The first blues
  tracks: Slow Burn (slow blues in G) and Boogie Shoes (jump blues in C).
- **2026-10-28.** "Replace those 2 tracks with the 3 new tracks… add a MATCHING melody line

  the record's vibe: the melody is written in the record's own voice, where the record leaves
  room.

## Sound

- **2026-10-29.** The dub techno track "is still not good at all… make the version in the game
  sound the same" as his reference render. The lesson for every track: a reference is a target
  to measure against, not a description; levels are set from renders, bus by bus.
- **2026-10-31.** Slow Burn at 68 is "too boring… WAY FASTER" at 90 in Adaptive, and he wanted
  the faster one: **90 is the slow blues**, and "in game mode ALL songs should play at their 1x
  tempo". Never slow a band to make a level easier.
- **2026-10-31.** The notes the user plays "should be distinct from the sound of the baked-in
  instruments": the player's monitor is not the band's comping instrument by default; Settings
  → Sound → Your instrument (piano / organ / Rhodes).
- **2026-10-31 → 2026-11-01.** The synthesized Hammond: "it's TERRIBLE. sounds like the
  dumbest instrument ever made." Then, on the sampled one: "this sounds alright - samples,
  right? … so why not do samples for a rhodes too." **Samples, never synthesized imitations of
  real instruments** (the Berlin synth voices are their own thing, not imitations).
- **2026-11-01.** "make 2 new blues tracks with a slightly different musical taste, more like
  super old school blues" (Robert Johnson's Delta, Muddy Waters' Chicago) "without any charts"
  → Dockery and Maxwell Street. Old school is instrumentation and device (one guitar and a foot;
  stop time with a unison riff), not a slower tempo.
- **2026-11-01.** "Dockery needs some more dirt. maybe a kick drum, or a little louder chords or
  something... make it hit harder" → a kick under the foot, the chick louder, a cross-stick slap
  on the backbeat, the bass doubling the thumb an octave down, the hands playing hard; RMS from
  −26.5 to −21.7, the peak −1.0. Then "try the sampled steel string guitar - add it to dockery -
  make it dirty" → the whole track on Shinyguitar at its hardest layers.
- **2026-11-01.** "make another delta blues track, still in the style of Robert Johnson… a
  louder or faster kind" → Friars Point, the boogie thumb under the slide's triplets at 116.
- **2026-11-07.** "make a high energy blues track that uses our new guitar and also has a keys
  track" → Halsted, Chicago boogie at 148, the electric through the amp, a rolling piano. Then,
  asking for its charts: "which sounds great btw". The amp settings and the seven-chorus
  arrangement are the reference for a loud track.
- **2026-11-07.** On the pinned tracks: the first six of the catalog show on the Stage, the rest
  under All tracks; a new track goes under All tracks unless he says pin it.

## The parts (what the player is handed)

- **2026-10-30.** Slow Burn melody on Easy "charted like a Medium"; Beginner notes "half length
  and don't attach correctly". Weights are what the levels are cut from: an ornament taken as
  an anchor is a stub on Beginner.
- **2026-10-31.** "melody / easy on Slow Burn is 2x too hard"; "slow burn, chords, easy: remove
  (almost) half of the chords". Easy is the anchors alone (about one a bar) on a melody and two
  hits a bar on a comping part. Write the weights for that.
- **2026-10-31.** "make a reference sheet for BLUES tracks… guidance for melody and chord
  gameplay difficulty, for each level… for all game charts" → `BLUES.md`. Every new track's
  figures go in its tables.
- **2026-11-01.** On the Delta tracks: "give them each melody and chords parts to be played on
  keys… make sure to use our .md file that has the technical details of how to do the
  difficulty leveling." A track without charts is not finished.
- **2026-11-02.** Chords on Easy, Dockery and Friars Point: "i'm not seeing traditional chords,
  instead i see bass notes"; he wants two-note chords from Easy, "playing on the off beats is
  fine". The chick-first weights.
- **2026-11-05.** Blue Lantern II on Medium: "too hard - make it at least 1/3 easier… by having
  simpler patterns that repeat more often." A first song is one riff.
- **2026-11-06.** Then: keep that chart as "EZ-2" and make a Medium "a little harder: 2 or 3
  moments in the song where it requires a hand repositioning." Difficulty climbs by figures
  that move the hand, a few, each coming home.
- **2026-11-07.** On Halsted: "make beginner, easy, medium charts for the new track." A fast
  song takes one anchor every two bars, and its reductions must stay fingerable.
- **2026-11-08.** On the teacher's solo track: "the FINGER MOVEMENTS required… CONSTANTLY
  telling me to move my hands around… at level 1, maybe I have to move my hand 4 times in the
  course of playing the full song, NOT 20+." The hand is the second axis of difficulty
  (`LEVELING.md`); a melody written for a level is measured on it.
