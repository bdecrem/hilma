# Now What? — CASBS project scratchpad

Living idea log for Bart's CASBS fellowship. Low-friction by design: dump raw
ideas, conversation notes, artifact sketches, and references here as they come.
Promote anything that hardens into its own doc. Kept in `docs/casbs/` so it
travels across machines.

> **Project recap.** Theme: *"Now what?"* — what's on the other side of AGI.
> Assume superintelligence by ~2028; intelligence on tap. The fellowship
> explores how to harness it so people *thrive* rather than fall into cognitive
> **offloading**, **debt**, and **surrender** ("a bicycle with wings" vs.
> "carbon-based DoorDash drivers for the AI"). Output shape: **ten
> conversations → ten artifacts** — each conversation with a CASBS fellow /
> researcher becomes a blog or podcast episode plus a built prototype/tool.
> Landing page + full statement: `src/app/nowwhat/about/`.
