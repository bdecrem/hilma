## Overview (moved from the root CLAUDE.md, 2026-10-22)

Where it lives: `src/app/design/<name>/` + `scripts/design/`. Deploy: Vercel (`ola.cx/design/<name>`).

Interactive, unbranded design specs for a product idea, hosted here instead of on claude.ai: a live tappable phone prototype of every screen, clickable flow maps built from the real screens scaled down, design notes per screen, a tokens panel with accent swatches, and a handoff list of what the brand designer owns. First one, 2026-10-09: `daily-call` (the Polly-as-a-daily-call idea: a three-minute spoken conversation a day started from a text; 11 screens, 3 flows), neutral on purpose: grays, Inter, one `--dc-accent` token. Same day, `daily-call-v2` is the high-fidelity pass Bart asked for so a junior designer has a vibe to react to: three parts a day (the call, three things to say back, a ten-question flash card game with no voice: pick five, type five, a × that leaves with the place kept and a paused map state), a map with a streak as the home, no setup screens; the phone plays a shortened day (`WALK` in content.ts: one of the three things, questions 1 and 6 of the ten) so a click-through takes a minute; **three styled walkthroughs** (2026-10-09, for a junior designer to react to): `daily-call-arcade` (neon on deep space, a pixel parrot, Bungee), `daily-call-candy` (pastel sky, jelly buttons, a gummy parrot, Baloo 2) and `daily-call-sticker` (zine white, fat outlines, hard shadows, tilted stickers, Bangers + Permanent Marker), each a slides page (`src/app/design/_walk/Slides.tsx`) around one shared engine: `_walk/Walk.tsx` is the v2 screens generated with `--w-*` tokens, `_walk/walk.css` the base, `_walk/<theme>.css` the skin, `_walk/Mascot.tsx` three parrots on the same motion hooks. The screen layer inside the phone needs an explicit width (Chromium sized it to the viewport inside the scaled frame). Check: `node scripts/design/walk-check.mjs <dir> <arcade|candy|sticker>`; the v2 page shows the three as a "Three looks" section with live previews (its layout loads every theme's fonts under per-theme variable names, `--font-arcade-display` etc.); **the v2 page was restructured the same evening into four numbered chapters** (copy ~2,400 → ~580 words): 1 The day (the phone beside one list of the five steps, thumbnail + name + one line, which replaces the old loop strip and screen notes; below 980px the list hides and the phone carries a caption), 2 The look (eight color chips, three type specimens, four parrot moods), 3 Other directions (the three skins as art-only tiles, a swipe row on phones), 4 Dodo, for reference ("Borrow the craft, not the cast."; `Dodo.tsx`, a one-phone gallery that taps through the 18 real Dodo screens from `@/app/dodo/scenes`), 5 The cast ("On second thought, borrow the cast."; three wordless jelly slots the one dodo and all fifteen critters take turns popping into, plus a "See all sixteen" link to `/design/daily-call-v2/cast`, everyone side by side with names; both animate only while on screen), art rendered at 1024 px from the `misc/dodo-redesign` canvas pages (no SVGs exist) by `scripts/dodo-jelly/web.mjs` into `public/dodo/jelly/` (still + squish frame, 512 and 1024 px WebP; the render hook is shared with the app's `sprites.mjs` through `hook.mjs`) — this material lives on the design package, not on dodo.foo; riso palette on paper, Bricolage Grotesque + Instrument Serif italic for the language, a geometric parrot (`Parrot.tsx`, four moods) as the placeholder mascot.
