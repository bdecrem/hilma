Mozilla.ai is a public benefit corporation (MZL.AI, PBC, San Francisco) backed by the Mozilla Foundation. It was founded in March 2023 with $30 million from Mozilla, started as a research lab, and pivoted in 2025 to building revenue products. CEO John Dickerson. The team is distributed across nine countries.

## Home page

We're building a future where AI works for you. At Mozilla.ai, we believe the next generation of AI solutions should be trustworthy, transparent, and controllable. That's why we're creating the infrastructure and products to build intelligent systems that align with your goals.

**Otari** (a Mozilla.ai product): control your LLM infrastructure from a single layer. Otari gives engineering teams one open-source control plane to route, budget, optimise costs, and secure LLM traffic across providers, without vendor lock-in. It's OSS, so you can self-host and keep full control.

**Octonous** (a Mozilla.ai product): make AI useful for your everyday work and focus on the work that matters. Describe your task and let Octonous execute it across your connected tools. Choose the AI model you already work with. Review and approve each action before it runs. Set triggers or schedules to run tasks automatically. Search documents across your connected apps. AI shouldn't replace your work. It should enhance it.

**Open tools.** Our open-source libraries provide a unified foundation for AI, supporting flexible agent orchestration, model selection, evaluation, or local single-file execution without runtimes.

- cq: a shared knowledge store that agents query before they act, turning individual learnings into collective intelligence across your entire agent ecosystem.
- any-llm: switch between LLM providers with a single configuration change.
- any-guardrail: a common interface for guardrail models, allowing you to quickly change between different models.
- mcpd: manage MCP servers across multiple environments.
- llamafile: distribute and run LLMs locally through a single-file executable, with no installation required.
- encoderfile: package transformer-based encoders into a fast, portable binary that runs anywhere with no runtime overhead.
- Mozilla.ai Blueprints: a community-led collection of reusable automation blueprints, integrating open-source tools and models, for developers, learners, and AI tinkerers.

## About

We're building AI you can trust in the workplace. At Mozilla.ai, we believe AI should empower people and organizations, not limit their choices or reduce their control. Rooted in Mozilla's commitment to openness, trust and human agency, we develop practical technologies that enable developers and organizations to build, deploy and operate AI systems on their own terms, with the flexibility to work across models, providers, and infrastructure. Mozilla.ai is backed by the Mozilla Foundation.

**Mission.** Our mission is to build open source AI products and infrastructure that give people and organisations greater choice, transparency and control over how they use AI.

**Values.** Trust by design (observability, safety, and human oversight baked in). Open foundations (we contribute to and build on open source, without dogma). Learning together (we test, listen, and improve in the open). Agency (AI should empower people and teams, not replace them). Focus (we cut complexity and put clarity before noise).

**Team.** A distributed team of engineers, product thinkers, researchers, and open-source builders, based across nine countries, representing 10 nationalities. "Rooted in Mozilla's values, we are a focused, fast-moving SaaS company working toward product-market fit with discipline." Named on the page: Sally Coome, Chief of Staff; Alejandro Gonzalez, VP Engineering. The board includes leaders from Mozilla and figures in AI, safety, governance, and open infrastructure.
