A road, not a dotted line

The trail is drawn as a real path with an edge and a worn center. Segments you've walked are solid and carry the dodo's footprints. The segment to the next level is stepping stones. Everything past that dissolves into fog. One glance tells you where you are and how far the road goes.

Today
Uniform 7pt dots between every node, same for cleared and locked.
Proposed
Three path states: worn, stepping stones, fogged. Footprints on worn stretches.

Stones with weight

Level nodes become chunky tiles: a lit top face over a darker base, sitting in a soft ground shadow. Stars sit in an arc carved into the stone, with empty sockets for the ones you haven't earned yet. Missing stars become something to go back for.

Today
Flat circles with a drop shadow, stars as three tiny glyphs under the node.
Proposed
Two-tone 3D tiles, star arc with sockets, current tile gets a bouncing map pin.

Landmarks make it a place

Each region gets one hero set piece the road passes: a turning windmill and a drifting balloon in the Meadow, a waterfall with a rope bridge in the Hollow (the road actually crosses the gorge), an observatory sweeping its beam over a campfire at the Summit. You remember where level 17 is because it's the bridge.

Today
Trees, flowers, a bunny, fireflies, a campfire. Lovely, but nothing to navigate by.
Proposed
One landmark per region, on the road, animated.

Milestones with their own shape

Every tenth level is a Gate: a larger crowned stone under an arch that names the next region. Every fifth is a rest stop with a signpost that reads your score in that region. A small chest sits beside a few levels in each region, closed until you clear that level. Three node types instead of one gives the trail rhythm.

Today
All 30 nodes identical apart from state.
Proposed
Gates at 10/20/30, rest stops at 5/15/25, chests at 3, 7 and 12, 16 and 22, 26.

Fog of discovery

Locked levels sit in a bank of fog that thins as you approach; the next locked stone is half revealed, the ones beyond are silhouettes. The Summit's stars show through the haze before you get there, so the mountain feels earned. Clearing a level visibly pushes the fog back.

Today
Locked nodes are grey circles with a padlock, fully visible to the horizon.
Proposed
Fog bank with a breathing edge at the frontier; locks only on the next two stones.

Depth and weather

Far hills scroll slower than near ones, so the world has depth when you move through it. Each region gets its own weather: petals drifting across the Meadow, leaves in the Hollow, stardust at the Summit. The sun in the finale hands off to the moon as you climb. The HUD ribbon shows region, position and star count without leaving the map.

Today
Single flat scroll, sparse ambient sprites.
Proposed
Three parallax layers, per-region particles, floating HUD ribbon.

Build notes

  • Everything here is Canvas, matching how the map is drawn today. It ports into PeckWorldCanvas and the node builder in FlashTabView without new assets. Roughly a day.
  • The region transition choreography (Clear / Walk / Gate / Settle) already exists and fits the new Gate stones as-is.
  • Reduce Motion freezes the ambience, as today. Sound and haptics on chest open and star collect would be the next layer, not in this mockup.
