Dodo · voice session · the face, two more ways

Let one instrument be the character

The v2 face tried to be a dodo and a control panel at once, and the knob ended up as a nose. These two drop the face entirely. Each puts a single real instrument where the face was, and the instrument's behavior is the expression: what it shows while Dodo listens, while you talk, and while Dodo speaks. The body, grille, feet and sprout stay from v2.

Concept A

The dial

A glass tuning window across the top, the way a 1960s Braun or Grundig set had. Printed on the band: LISTEN on the left, a marigold TALK zone on the right, tiny dodo footprints for tick marks. One red needle.

The needle is the whole performance. It rests on LISTEN. Hold to talk and it swings into the marigold zone and quivers with your voice. Release and it drifts back while Dodo thinks, then trembles on LISTEN as Dodo speaks. Nothing is a face, yet the thing clearly has a mood.

  • Hold-to-talk: the speaker section is replaced by one raised key that fills the center of the radio. Ink face, marigold rim, a mic glyph and PRESS TO TALK engraved on it. It is the only thing on the body that looks pressable, and it is.
  • Engaged: the key sinks, turns solid marigold with a halo, the legend flips to LISTENING, the needle swings into TALK and the dial glass lights. Release: the key returns to ink, ONE MOMENT while Dodo thinks. While Dodo speaks the legend reads PRESS TO CUT IN.
  • Under the radio, small: "Press and hold the button to talk."
  • Hands-free: the perforated speaker stays. The needle idles at LISTEN and shivers with Dodo's voice. Mute is the small switch on the grille's shoulder.
  • Illustrator's note: the band is printed slightly off-register, like a real silkscreened dial.
Concept B

The scope

One round oscilloscope screen, ink glass with a marigold trace. A cyclops radio. Idle, the trace draws a gentle arc, a closed-eye smile, which is the one wink in the whole design. Speak, and it becomes your live waveform. Dodo speaks, and it becomes Dodo's. Thinking is a slow dotted sweep.

This is the more graphic of the two and the more animated: the trace is real audio, so the character is literally made of the conversation.

  • Hold-to-talk target: the whole radio. Held, the screen's bezel ring lights marigold and the trace goes bright.
  • Hands-free: the bezel stays dark; the trace alone carries the state.
  • Illustrator's note: a faint phosphor grid behind the trace, and a small TX lamp beside the screen so transmit still has a hardware tell.
