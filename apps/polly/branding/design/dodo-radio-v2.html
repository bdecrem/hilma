Dodo · voice session · art concept v2

A small, well-made radio that happens to be a dodo

Same silhouette and palette as today, less nursery. The face becomes hardware: the eyes are two status lamps, the beak is the tuning knob, the grille is a real perforated speaker with a VU meter behind it. In hold-to-talk mode the grille swaps for one big soft key that fills the device, so the whole radio is the button.

Hands-free

The default. Two lamps idle, the meter breathes while Dodo listens and jumps when it speaks. Mute lives on the dial: pressed in, the knob turns ink.

Hold to talk

The speaker becomes the key. Press anywhere on the radio: the body sinks, the TX lamp goes marigold, the ring lights, and the meter follows your voice. Release and it hands the turn to Dodo.

Hold-to-talk states

One object, four moods. The key's legend and the two lamps carry the state, so no sentence under the radio has to.

What changed from v1

  • Cartoon eyes and blush are gone. Two lamps in the same positions keep the face readable without the plush look.
  • The beak is now a knurled marigold knob with a pointer. It's the only saturated element on the body.
  • Bone-colored body with a slight top-light gradient, a shadow line at the base, and four dark rubber feet instead of marigold ones.
  • Real perforated grille. The VU meter sits behind it, so the bars read as light through metal, not stickers.
  • Label plate in small mono caps, engraved rather than a yellow tape. The sprout stays, smaller and off-center.
  • Backdrop calms down: a single soft sun glow, no clouds.

Hold-to-talk as a physical object

The hit target is the entire radio, not a pill under it. That reads as a walkie-talkie: you grab the thing and squeeze. While held the body drops 6pt and its shadow tightens, the key face lifts a shade lighter with a marigold ring, the right lamp turns marigold as TX, and the meter tracks input level. On release the ring fades, the lamp blinks twice while Dodo thinks, then both lamps go steady and the meter goes big for the reply. Holding during a reply cuts it off: the meter collapses in one beat and TX lights.

  • Legend on the key: a mic glyph and HOLD in mono caps. Held: LISTENING. Thinking: three dots. Speaking: the legend fades and the meter takes over.
  • Haptic on press and release, a soft double tick on cutoff.
  • End stays a separate red control below. Nothing else on screen.
