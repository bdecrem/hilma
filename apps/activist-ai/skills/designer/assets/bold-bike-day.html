Eastgate Library and the Spoke Collective

Free bike fix day

Bring your bike, or your kid's. Volunteer mechanics fix it with you, for free, while you wait.

Saturday, May 2, 10 am to 3 pm
Eastgate Library parking lot

What we fix

  • Flat tiresNew tubes are free, up to 60 of them.
  • BrakesPads, cables, and squeaks.
  • ChainsCleaned, oiled, or swapped.
  • GearsSo it shifts when you ask it to.
  • SeatsRaised for kids who grew this winter.
  • LightsA free set for the first 40 riders.

How it works

Get a number

At the pink tent by the library doors. No sign-up needed.

Fix it together

A mechanic shows you how, so you can do it yourself next time.

Ride home

Most fixes take 20 minutes. Story time runs inside for kids at 11.
