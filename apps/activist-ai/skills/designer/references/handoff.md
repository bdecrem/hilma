# Stage 8: hand off

Tell the person three short sentences, in plain words, and nothing more unless they ask:

1. **The idea:** what the page is and why. ("The page is a poster for the meeting: the date is the
   biggest thing on it, and the agenda reads like a timetable.")
2. **What to look at:** where to open it and the one thing to check. ("Open index.html on your phone
   and check the address and the time.")
3. **What I would do next:** one concrete next step, including any draft lines to confirm.
   ("Next I would add the building's floor plan; the line about childcare is a draft, please confirm it.")

If you could not look at the page with a model (no `ola-see`), add one sentence saying so.

## The files you leave

- `index.html` (and its other pages), `fonts/` with the licenses, the page's CSS
- `design/notes.md` (the brief, the idea, the choice in two sentences)
- `design/a.html`, `design/b.html`, `design/sheet.png` (the directions you compared)
- `.shots/` (the rounds at phone and laptop width, hidden from the project list; open them yourself)
