scores.csv has scores (0 to 100) for twelve countries in five categories, for 2020 and 2023. Build a small static website from it, working offline with no outside files:

1. index.html: every country, ranked from the highest to the lowest average 2023 score (the average of the five categories, one decimal), each country's name linking to its own page, plus a chart of the 2023 averages (an inline SVG or a canvas).
2. countries/<code>.html for each country (code is the country's code from the file): the five category scores for 2020 and 2023 and the change for each.

The index must read well on a phone (390 px wide, no sideways scrolling).
