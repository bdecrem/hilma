Build a small, original browser game called "Paper Boat". Write it as one file, index.html, in this folder: HTML, CSS and JavaScript together, drawn on a canvas, no build step, no libraries, no web fonts, no images or sounds from the network.

The game: seen from above, a folded paper boat drifts down a river at night. The river scrolls toward the player; the boat stays near the bottom of the screen and moves only left and right. Rocks come down the river and must be dodged: touching one ends the run. Floating paper lanterns can be collected: each one adds to the score and glows a little brighter than the last. The river slowly speeds up and gets busier the longer the run lasts, so a run is easy for the first ten seconds and hard after a minute. The banks of the river are the left and right limits.

Controls: on a keyboard, the left and right arrow keys or A and D steer, Space or Enter starts and restarts, P pauses. On a touch screen, dragging a finger left and right anywhere steers the boat (the boat follows the finger), and a tap starts and restarts. Steering must feel smooth: the boat eases toward where it is going and leans a little into the turn.

Screens: a start screen with the name of the game, one line saying how to play, and the best score; the game itself with the score at the top; a game-over screen with the score, the best score (kept in localStorage) and how to play again. The game pauses by itself when the tab is hidden.

Sound: a few small sounds made in code with WebAudio (a soft chime for a lantern, a low thud for a rock), starting only after the first key press or tap, with a mute button in a corner.

Design: this must look like the work of a professional designer with taste, not a template. One visual idea: everything is cut paper lit by lantern light. A deep ink-blue river with slow ripples, a cream paper boat with a visible fold, warm sunflower-yellow lanterns that light the water around them, dark paper rocks with a pale edge, reeds on the banks. No emoji, no purple gradients, no neon. Confident typography with system fonts. A wake behind the boat and a small burst of light when a lantern is collected. It must fill the whole screen, run smoothly at 60 frames a second, look right on a phone held upright (390 px wide) and on a desktop, stay sharp on high-density screens (cap the device pixel ratio at 2), and set a theme-color meta tag that matches the river.

For testing, the page must set window.game to an object with two fields kept up to date: state (one of "start", "playing", "paused", "over") and score (a number).

When the file is written, read it once more and fix any JavaScript error. Finish by saying in one sentence what you built.
