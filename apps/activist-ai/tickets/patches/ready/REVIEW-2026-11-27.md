Nothing was applied to the repo, committed or deployed. The checks ran in a throwaway worktree of main (37532267), which has since been removed.

## Verdicts

2. **OLA-426 (Kestrel page not published): DO NOT MERGE the winner.** Its `\bon ola\b` brings back the OLA-244 bug: "change the words on my page on ola to Rowan" no longer gets the "not public yet" rule. Merge the combined pattern below instead.

## 1. OLA-387: `frontdesk/faults.mjs` refusalOf

**The test.** Qwen's test is the ticket's own six sentences, plus two guards ("…says Bart.", "You can open it here." stay null), plus the path through readTurn and keptKinds. It does test what the ticket says. It is narrow, though, because it is literal: a fix can add those exact phrases and pass. All three tries did roughly that. The test passes while near variants are still missed:
- "There is no way for me to send texts." (missed by tries 1 and 2)
- "Sending texts is beyond what I can do." (missed by all three)
- "I couldn't find…" (never counted, even on main)

The `here's` half has the same gap. "I can't send emails myself, but **here is** a draft." is still labeled cannot_here by every try. That is the same bug with the apostrophe spelled out.

**The tries.** On main, the refusal test plus faults.test.mjs passes 8 of 11. Each of the three tries passes 11 of 11. I fed each version extra sentences:

- **Try 1 (winner).** It works, but:
  - It drops the `\b` after `here` (`here(?!')`).
  - It adds a nonsense alternative, `i can't be able to`.
  - It adds `have no (?:a |the |any )?way` ("have no a way"). This also counts a successful answer such as "The page is up. We have no way of knowing who visited it" as no_tool.
  - It adds a duplicate `from this (…machine|computer)` group.
- **Try 2.** Cleaner, because it keeps the `\b`. It has the same "we have no way of knowing" false positive, and it misses "That's not something I can do."
- **Try 3. Worst.** It adds `can only` to CANT, so successful answers count as refusals: "Done. You can only see the page when you are signed in here." and "I can only find two files in this project: …" both become cannot_here.

**Other copies of the rule.** `evals/asks/lib.mjs` NEVER has its own "cannot from here" pattern for grading evals. It does not use bare `here` and is not affected. No other caller uses refusalOf.

**The change to merge** (`review-ola-387.diff`; with Qwen's test, 11 of 11 pass):
- no_tool: add `way`, `\bi have no way\b` and `\bno way for me to\b`.
- cannot_here: `here\b` becomes `here\b(?!'| is\b)`.
- cannot: add `\bi won't be able to\b`, `\b(?:isn't|not) something i can do\b` and `\boutside what i can do\b`.

With these, "here is a draft" counts as a plain cannot, and "we have no way of knowing" stays null. One false positive remains, and it is cheap: "I won't be able to resist adding a footer… Done." counts as cannot. It only adds noise to a counter.

## 2 and 3. OLA-426 and OLA-407: `ASKS_PUBLISH` in `frontdesk/publishednote.mjs` and `gateway/publishedchange.mjs`

Both bugs are the same pattern. Qwen read that correctly. Both copies have to stay equal, and `gateway/test.mjs` checks that they do.

**The tests.** Both test what the tickets say, using the tickets' own sentences. Both guard the OLA-244 cases ("make the heading say Willow instead" still gets the note). They prove one thing only: the "does not ask to publish" note is no longer added. They do not prove that Ola then publishes.

For OLA-407 that difference matters. A third copy of "asks to publish" exists: the publish cue in `workspace/plugins/ola-skillcue/index.js` (CUES), which loads the publish skill. I ran it on the ticket's sentences:

| Sentence | Publish skill loaded today? |
|---|---|
| "now change the page so it says Damson and put it on ola again" | no |
| "ok now make it say Sloe and stick it on ola again" | no |
| "hmm can u change it to say Hazel n put it up on ola again pls" (the one that published) | yes |
| "put something up on ola … Kestrel" (OLA-426) | yes |

So for OLA-426, removing the note is likely the whole fix. For OLA-407, removing the note takes away the "do not publish" instruction, but the publish skill still does not load for "put/stick it on ola". OLA-230 and OLA-284 showed what happens then: the model publishes without the skill's prepare and check, or does not publish at all. No patch touched skillcue.

**Test results.** On main, the 426 test passes 2 of 3 and the 407 test 3 of 4. Every try passes its own test. Every try fails the other bug's test:
- Each 407 try fails the 426 test, because "put something up on ola" is still read as a change.
- Each 426 try fails the 407 test. "put it on the web" is missed by all three, and "stick it on ola again" by tries 2 and 3 as well.

publishednote.test.mjs passes 4 of 4 and gateway/test.mjs 63 of 63 with every try.

**False positives** (sentences that are only changes, which must keep the note):
- **426 try 1** (`\bon ola\b`, the winner) drops the note for "change the words on my page on ola to Rowan", "fix the typo on the page on ola.cx", "make the heading on ola say Willow" and "add a line: we are on ola now". That is the OLA-244 bug coming back: Ola edits the publish copy or publishes a change it was not asked to publish. This is the reason not to merge it.
- **426 try 2** (`put … on ola` within 80 characters) misses "stick".
- **426 try 3** is narrow and fine, but misses "stick" and "on the web".
- **407 try 1** adds `lay` and "on the site" (as in "put the date on the site", which is a change). Both are needless.
- **407 try 3** adds `set` and `post`. "set it up so the heading is bigger" then counts as a request to publish.

**The change to merge for both tickets** (`review-ola-407-426.diff`, the same line in both files):

```
export const ASKS_PUBLISH = /\bpublish|\bunpublish|\b(?:put|get|bring|stick)\s+(?:it\s+|this\s+|that\s+|something\s+|anything\s+|a\s+\w+\s+|the\s+\w+\s+)?(?:back\s+)?(?:up|online|live|on\s+(?:ola|the\s+(?:web|internet)))\b|\bgo(?:es)?\s+live\b|\bmake\s+(?:it|this|the\s+\w+)\s+(?:public|live)\b|\btake\s+(?:it\s+|this\s+|the\s+\w+\s+)?down\b|\blink\s+anyone\b/i
```

It is 407 try 1 without `lay` and "the site", plus the objects from 426 try 3 ("something", "anything", "a page"). With it:
- Both of Qwen's tests pass (11 of 11), plus publishednote.test.mjs, gateway/test.mjs (63 of 63) and chatpublish.test.mjs (16 of 16).
- None of the change-only sentences above lose the note.
- One known edge remains: "change the title of the page I put on ola yesterday" loses the note, because the object is optional.

Commit Qwen's two test files with it: `frontdesk/test/publishwords.test.mjs` and `gateway/publishwords.test.mjs`. They are on the Studio, under `~/ola-trials/write-tests/2026-11-27T22-38-38/work/…`.
