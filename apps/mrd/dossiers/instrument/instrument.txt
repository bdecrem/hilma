           Migrant Rights Initiative Migrant Rights Database
                    Coding Instrument (2023 Data Collection)
The Migrant Rights Initiative’s Migrant Rights Database (MRD) is the first global data source
benchmarking the extent to which states fulfill international obligations in national law, as reflected in
the Initiative’s International Migrants Bill of Rights (IMBR), and how they implement these
protections. The database applies a novel instrument that permits the objective, cross-national
accounting of the laws protecting migrants’ rights enshrined in national legal frameworks. This is the
instrument used for the Initiative’s 2023 MRD data collection. For more information visit
www.migrantrightsinitiative.org.
