# Dossier H — Russia


## Civil and political life

### q91 · Right of regular migrants to vote in local and/or regional elections.
- Law: No right to vote in any election
- Implementation: Does Not Apply

No protection of this right. Article 32 of the Constitution of the Russian Federation gives "the right to elect and be elected to state bodies of power and local self-government bodities" and to "participate in referenda" only to Russian citizens. Suffrage for permanently residing foreign citizens is theoretically possible but has never been implemented in the Russian Federation: Article 12 of the Federal Law “On the legal status of foreign citizens in the Russian Federation” “Attitude of foreign citizens to the right to vote” 1. Foreign citizens in the Russian Federation shall not have the right to elect and be elected to federal bodies of state power, bodies of state power of constituent entities of the Russian Federation, as well as to participate in a referendum of the Russian Federation and referendums of constituent entities of the Russian Federation. 2. Foreign citizens permanently residing in the Russian Federation, in the cases and in the manner prescribed by federal laws, have the right to elect and be elected to local self government bodies, as well as to participate in a local referendum.

### q93 · Right of regular migrants to stand for election.
- Law: No right to run for public office in any election
- Implementation: Does Not Apply

No protection of this right. Article 32 of the Constitution of the Russian Federation gives "the right to elect and be elected to state bodies of power and local self-government bodities" and to "participate in referenda" only to Russian citizens. Article 12 of the Federal Law “On the legal status of foreign citizens in the Russian Federation", “Attitude of foreign citizens to the right to vote”: "1. Foreign citizens in the Russian Federation shall not have the right to elect and be elected to federal bodies of state power, bodies of state power of constituent entities of the Russian Federation, as well as to participate in a referendum of the Russian Federation and referendums of constituent entities of the Russian Federation..."


## Due process

### q35 · Right to equal protection of the law before courts.
- Law: Yes
- Implementation: Usually; Sometimes

National law in the Russian Federation protects this right in the Constitution of the Russian Federation: Article 19 which states: "1. All people shall be equal before the law and court. 2. The State shall guarantee the equality of rights and freedoms of man and citizen, regardless of sex, race, nationality, language, origin, property and official status, place of residence, religion, convictions, membership of public associations, and also of other circumstances. All forms of limitations of human rights on social, racial, national, linguistic or religious grounds shall be banned." According to Article 25.1 of the Code of the Russian Federation on Administrative Offenses of December 30, 2001 N 195-FZ (the rights of a person in respect of whom proceedings are being conducted in an administrative offense are granted to everyone equally, regardless of the presence of citizenship of the Russian Federation. The national law of the Russian Federation protects this right in relation to the criminal court in accordance with: Article 19 of the Constitution of the Russian Federation; Article 42, 46, 47. Code of Criminal Procedure of the Russian Federation (gives the same rights to all victims, accused, suspects, regardless of their citizenship of the Russian Federation); Section 13, 15 of the Code of Criminal Procedure of the Russian Federation (gives the same rights to appeal against court verdicts, regardless of the presence of citizenship of the Russian Federation). The national law of the Russian Federation protects this right in relation to the civil court in: Article 19 of the Constitution of the Russian Federation; Article 36 of the Civil Procedure Code of Russia “Civil Procedural Legal Capacity”: Civil procedural legal capacity is equally recognized for all citizens and organizations that, in accordance with the legislation of the Russian Federation, have the right to judicial protection of rights, freedoms and legitimate interests.

### q39 · Right to government-provided interpretation in criminal proceedings.
- Law: Yes
- Implementation: Sometimes; Effectively Always

National law in Russia have a right to interpretation at government expense in criminal proceedings, in: 1) Part 2 of the article 18 of the Criminal Procedure Code of the Russian Federation, No. 174-FZ of December 18, 2001: "To participants in criminal court proceedings who have no command or just a poor command of the language in which the proceedings in the criminal case are conducted, shall be explained and guaranteed the right to make statements, to give explanations and testimony, to lodge petitions and complaints, to get acquainted with the materials of the criminal case and to take the floor in the court using their native tongue or another language of which they have a good command, and to make use free of charge of interpreter's services in accordance with the procedure established by the present Code." 2) Criminal Procedure Code of the Russian Federation of December 18, 2001, No. 174-FZ: Article 59 "Interpreter." 3) Federal Constitutional Law "On Judicial System of the Russian Federation" of December 31, 1996, No. 1-FKZ: Article 10 "The language of court proceedings."

### q41 · Right to be protected from prosecution for unauthorized entry.
- Law: No
- Implementation: Does Not Apply

No protection of this right. Migrants can be held criminally and administratively liable for unauthorized entry into the Russian Federation in accordance with Article 322 of the Russian Federation Criminal Code (June 13, 1996), N63-FZ: "Illegal Crossing of the Russian Federation state border." An exception to the general rule is the unauthorized entry of victims of human trafficking.

## Expulsion, asylum, and non-refoulement

### q47 · Right to maintain immigration status after convictions.
- Law: any conviction (even minor administrative offenses) can lead to loss of residence status
- Implementation: Does Not Apply

No protection of this right. Russian law provides broad discretion for loss of residence status under a range of circumstances. 1) Article 26 Federal Law No. 114-FZof August 15, 1996 "On the Procedure for Departure from the Russian Federation and Entry into the Russian Federation:"With regard to migrants who have repeatedly (two or more times) within one year been brought to administrative responsibility for committing an administrative offense related to an encroachment on public order and public safety or in violation of the regime of stay (residence) of foreign citizens or stateless persons in the Russian Federation or the procedure for their labor activity on the territory of the Russian Federation, a decision may be made to ban entry into the Russian Federation. If a migrant is in the Russian Federation at the time such a decision is made, a decision is made to reduce the period of his stay and, as a result, the migrant is obliged to leave the Russian Federation. 2) Article 7 Federal Law No. 115-FZ of July 25, 2002 "On the Legal Status of Foreign Citizens in the Russian Federation.": Grounds for refusal to issue or annul a temporary residence permit. 3) Article 9 Federal Law of July 25, 2002 N 115-FZ "On the Legal Status of Foreign Citizens in the Russian Federation.": Grounds for refusal to issue or annul a residence permit The Federal Law "On the Russian Federation (RF) Citizenship" from May 31, 2002 N 62-FZ, Article 16: "Grounds for rejection of applications for receiving the Russian Federation (RF) citizenship, admission to the RF citizenship or for the restoration of the RF citizenship." A temporary residence permit is not issued or canceled if the Migrant: a) has been convicted by a court verdict that has entered into legal force for committing a grave or especially grave crime or for committing a crime related to illicit trafficking in narcotic drugs, psychotropic substances and their analogues or precursors, plants containing narcotic drugs or psychotropic substances or their precursors, as well as their parts containing narcotic drugs or psychotropic substances or their precursors; b) has an outstanding or unexpunged conviction for committing a grave or especially grave crime on the territory of the Russian Federation or outside it, recognized as such in accordance with federal law. For some administrative offenses, a migrant may be deprived of the status of TRP / residence permit, or a migrant may be denied these statuses. A migrant may be denied admission to Russian citizenship if they: a) have an unexpunged or outstanding conviction for committing intentional crimes on the territory of the Russian Federation or outside it, recognized as such in accordance with federal law; b) are prosecuted by the competent authorities of the Russian Federation or by the competent authorities of foreign states for crimes recognized as such in accordance with federal law (until a court verdict is passed or a decision is made on the case); c) have been convicted and are serving a sentence of imprisonment for actions prosecuted in accordance with federal law (until the expiration of the sentence).

### q49 · Right to not return to a risk of torture or inhumane treatment.
- Law: Yes
- Implementation: Rarely

National law in the Russian Federation protects this right in: 1) Article 12 of the Ruling of The Plenary Session of The Supreme Court of The Russian Federation (14.06.2012) No. 11 "On the practice of review by courts of cases related to extradition of persons for criminal pursuit or execution of criminal punishment or imprisonment.": “Courts shall consider that in accordance with article 7 of the The International Covenant on Civil and Political Rights in interpretation of the UN Committee on Human Rights and the article 3 of the Convention against Torture and Other Cruel, Inhuman or Degrading Treatment or Punishment Adopted and opened for signature, ratification and accession by General Assembly resolution 39/46 of 10 December 1984, "No State Party shall expel, return ("refouler") or extradite a person to another State where there are substantial grounds for believing that he would be in danger of being subjected to torture, or to inhuman or degrading treatment or punishment." 2) Article 4 of the Instructions approved by the Order of the Federal Security Service of the Russian Federation (23.12.2008) No. 631 "On approval of the Instruction on organization of actions of the border control bodies on administrative refoulement of foreign citizens and stateless persons from the territory of Russian Federation" states that: "A Foreign citizen shall not be expelled to another State where there are substantial grounds for believing that he would be in danger of being subjected to torture. For the purpose of determining whether there are such grounds, the competent authorities shall take into account all relevant considerations including, where applicable, the existence in the State concerned of a consistent pattern of gross, flagrant or mass violations of human rights." 3) Q 50(2) The Ruling Of The Plenary Session Of The Supreme Court cited in the above answer to this question refers to the extradition. It is common practice for the case law to refer to this ruling during the refoulement cases.

## Family

### q73 · Right of settled migrants to family reunification.
- Law: Conditional
- Implementation: Sometimes; Usually

National law in the Russian Federation conditionally protects this right in Federal Law No. 115-FZ of July 25, 2002, "On the Legal Position of Foreign Citizens in the Russian Federation." Conditionality is based on visa type of the sponsoring migrant, and the family members they can sponsor.

### q75 · Right to judicial remedy for denials of right to family reunification.
- Law: Yes
- Implementation: Sometimes; Rarely

National law in the Russian Federation protects this right in Article 46 of the Constitution of the Russian Federation which states: "1. Everyone shall be guaranteed the judicial protection of his rights and freedoms. 2. Decisions and actions (or inaction) of bodies of state authority and local self government, public associations and officials may be appealed against in court. 3. Everyone shall have the right to appeal, according to international treaties of the Russian Federation, to international bodies for the protection of human rights and freedoms, if all the existing internal state means of legal protection have been exhausted. The article 4 of the Administrative Court Proceedings Code of the Russian Federation from 08.03.2015 21-FZ: Each concerned person has a right to apply to a court for protection of violated or disputed rights, freedoms, and legitimate interests."

### q77 · Right to recognition of same-sex marital relationships.
- Law: No
- Implementation: Does Not Apply

No protection of this right. According to national law in Russia, same sex marital relationships are not recognized under the family reunification framework. Article 12 of the Family Code of The Russian Federation no. 223-fz (December 29, 1995) explains that "to enter into a marriage, the voluntary consent of the man and of the woman entering into it, and their reaching the marriageable age, shall be necessary."

### q79 · Right to family reunification without resource conditions
- Law: Yes
- Implementation: Usually

National law in the Russian Federation protects this right in the Federal Law No. 115-FZ of July 25, 2002, "On the Legal Position of Foreign Citizens in the Russian Federation." As a general rule, there are no economic requirements for temporary staying migrants in the Russian Federation, but upon obtaining various long-term statuses (temporary residence permit; residence permit etc.), there are requirements to provide evidence confirming the presence of a source of legal income in the Russian Federation, allowing them to suppose themselves.

### q137 · Right to citizenship for stateless migrants.
- Law: No
- Implementation: Does Not Apply

No protection of this right for all migrants. However, migrant children who are at risk of becoming stateless do have a right to acquire citizenship by birth under Article 12 of the Federal Law “On Citizenship of the Russian Federation” Acquisition of citizenship of the Russian Federation by birth : 1. A child acquires citizenship of the Russian Federation by birth if on the child's birthday: в) one of his parents has the citizenship of the Russian Federation, and the other parent is a foreign citizen, provided that the child was born on the territory of the Russian Federation or if otherwise he becomes a stateless person; г) both of his parents or his only parent residing on the territory of the Russian Federation are foreign citizens or stateless persons, provided that the child was born on the territory of the Russian Federation, and the state of which his parents or his only parent are citizens does not provide child his nationality. Adult migrants who are stateless and have legal capacity are entitled to apply for admission to the citizenship of the Russian Federation in a simplified manner without complying with the conditions for a five-year period of residence on the basis of a Residence Permit in the Russian Federation under Part 1, Article 14 of the Federal Law “On Citizenship of the Russian Federation.” After obtaining a Residence Permit, Stateless Persons have the right to immediately apply for citizenship but this is not guaranteed.


## Health

### q119 · Right of access to health care services.
- Law: Yes, but only for certain visa types and migrant statuses (e.g., economic, family, humanitarian, temporary or regular migrants)
- Implementation: Sometimes; Usually

National law in the Russian Federation protects this right in Article 19 of the Federal Law of November 21, 2012, No. 323-FZ "On Public Health Protection in the Russian Federation" : 3. The right to health care of foreign citizens living and staying in the territory of the Russian Federation, established by the Russian Federation legislation and relevant international treaties of the Russian Federation. Stateless persons permanently residing in the Russian Federation have the right to medical care on a par with Russian citizens, unless otherwise provided by international treaties of the Russian Federation. Article 5 Decree of the Government of the Russian Federation of March 6, 2013 N 186 "On approval of the Rules for the provision of medical care to foreign citizens on the territory of the Russian Federation" 5. Emergency, including emergency specialized, medical care is provided to foreign citizens in case of diseases, accidents, injuries, poisoning and other conditions requiring urgent medical intervention. Medical organizations of the state and municipal health care systems provide this medical care to foreign citizens free of charge. As a general rule, only emergency medical care is provided free of charge to temporarily staying foreign citizens. Planned medical care will not be provided free of charge. Refugees, persons who have received a temporary residence permit, a residence permit, have the opportunity to receive a wider range of medical services in Russia free of charge on the basis of Article 10 of the Federal Law of November 29, 2010 N 326-ФЗ “On Compulsory Medical Insurance in the Russian Federation.” Labor migrants from the countries of the Eurasian Economic Union (EAEU) have the right to receive medical care on an equal basis with Russian citizens in the case of official employment. Starting from 1 January 2023, foreign citizens temporary staying in the Russian Federation (except for highly qualified specialists) receive an access to health care services on an equal basis with Russian citizens, under the condition that the insurant has been paying insurance premium for them during the last three years (changes came into force on 1 January 2023 in accordance with Federal Law no.237-fz of14 July 2022).

## Labor

### q95 · Right to equal pay for the same work.
- Law: Yes
- Implementation: Sometimes; Rarely

National law in the Russian Federation protects this right in Article 3 of the Labor Code of The Russian Federation of 31 December 2001 Federal Law No. 197-fz Of 2001: "Everyone shall have equal opportunities to realize his/her labor rights. No one can be constrained in his/her labor rights and freedoms or get any advantages irrespective of sex, race, color of skin, nationality, language, origins, property, social or position status, age, domicile, religious beliefs, political convictions, affiliation or non-affiliation with public associations as well as other factors not relevant to professional qualities of the employee. Establishment of distinctions, exceptions, preferences as well as limitation of employees' rights which are determined by the requirements inherent in a specific kind of work as set by federal laws or caused by the especial attention of the state to the persons requiring increased social and legal protection shall not be deemed discrimination. The persons considering themselves to be discriminated against in the sphere of labor shall be entitled to petition the federal labor inspectorate bodies and/or courts applying for restoration of their violated rights, compensation of the material loss and redress of the moral damage."

### q97 · Right to freedom of choice of employment.
- Law: Workers can freely change employers within a specific sector
- Implementation: Usually; Sometimes

National law in the Russian Federation protects this right at different levels for different categories of migrants in Article 13.3 of the Federal Law of Russia # 115-FZ from 25.07.2002 On the Legal Position of Foreign Citizens in the Russian Federation. While foreign citizens of non-visa countries have a right to a free choice of employment within a specific region, other foreign citizens from countries with established visa regime are tied to a specific employer. According to Article 13.3: "Labor of foreign citizens from non-visa countries employed based on a patent 1. Employers or service providers registered as organizations or individual entrepreneurs, or notaries, attorneys acting in form of attorney's office, or other organizations which should be registered or licensed in accordance with federal laws have the right to attract to labor regulated foreign citizens arrived at the territory of the Russian Federation from non- visa countries, reached 18 years if such foreign citizen has a patent issued in accordance with respective Federal Law. 2.Employers or service providers who are citizens of the Russian Federation have the right to attract for provision of personal, domestic and other similar needs, not related to business activities, foreign citizens arrived at the territory of the Russian Federation from non- visa countries, reached 18 years if such foreign citizen has a patent issued in accordance with respective Federal Law. 15. If it is necessary for a foreign citizen to perform work in a profession (specialty, position, type of work) not specified in the patent, the foreign citizen has the right to apply to the territorial body of the federal executive body in the field of internal affairs, which issued the patent, for appropriate changes to the information contained in the patent. 16. A foreign citizen is not entitled to carry out work outside the limits of the subject of the Russian Federation on the territory of which he has been granted a patent. The employer or service provider is not entitled to involve a foreign citizen in employment under a patent outside the limits of the subject of the Russian Federation on the territory of which the foreign citizen has been issued a patent. In order to perform work on the territory of another subject of the Russian Federation, the foreign citizen is obliged to apply for a patent to the territorial body of the federal executive body in the field of internal affairs in the constituent entity of the Russian Federation, on the territory of which the foreign citizen intends to carry out work activities."

### q101 · Right to maintain immigration status after loss of employment.
- Law: Loss of employment implies loss of residence status unless a new job is found within a specified time period
- Implementation: Usually; Sometimes

National law in the Russian Federation protects this right, in a limited way, in part 5, article 5 of the Federal Law of Russia # 115-FZ from 25.07.2002 "On the Legal Position of Foreign Citizens in the Russian Federation." The period of temporary stay in the Russian Federation of a foreign citizen is extended upon issuance of a patent to a foreign citizen, upon extension of the validity of a patent Article 13.3. The term of a patent (work permit) can be repeatedly extended for a period of one month. At the same time, the total term of a patent, taking into account extensions, cannot be more than twelve months from the date of issue of the patent. The term of a patent is considered to be extended for the period for which personal income tax has been paid in the form of a fixed advance payment. It is important to note that an employer is not actually needed for a monthly patent renewal. The extension occurs by paying an advance taxpayment. After losing a job, if the migrant continues to pay the patent fee, the migration status will not be cancelled. However, migrants working under a patent have an obligation: within two months from the date of issuance of a patent, they are obliged to submit personally or send by registered mail with a return receipt to the territorial body of the federal executive body in the field of internal affairs that issued the patent, a copy of the employment contract or civil law contract for the performance of work (provision of services) (Clause 7 of Article 13.3 of Law N 115-FZ "On the Legal Status of Foreign Citizens in the Russian Federation"). In connection with non-fulfillment of this norm, the patent may be canceled and the migrant loses his migration status. If a visa migrant lost his job for the following reasons: - cancellation of the permission to attract and use foreign workers issued to the previous employer (customer of works, services); -termination of the activities of the employer (customer of works, services). Then, as a general rule, he has the right to conclude a new contract with another employer without losing his migration status. If the loss of a job is due to other circumstances, the migrant must obtain a new work permit. Otherwise, the status will be lost. For citizens of the Eurasian Economic Union (EAEU), there are greater protections in place as outlined in Article 97 of the Treaty on the Eurasian Economic Union" (Signed in Astana on May 29, 2014) / Labor activity of workers of the Member States 5. The period of temporary stay (residence) of a member state worker and family members in the territory of the state of employment is determined by the term of the labor or civil law contract concluded by the worker of the member state with the employer or customer of works (services). 9. In case of early termination of an employment or civil law contract after the expiration of 90 days from the date of entry into the territory of the state of employment, the worker of the Member State has the right to conclude a new labor or civil law contract within 15 days without leaving the territory of the state of employment.

### q103 · Right to equal employment conditions and protection.
- Law: Migrants have a right to most of the employment conditions and protections for citizens
- Implementation: Usually; Rarely

National law in the Russian Federation protects this right in article 11 of the Labor Code of The Russian Federation (31 December 2001) Federal Law No. 197-fz Of 2001. Part 4.1 article 2 Federal Law of December 29, 2006 N 255-FZ "On compulsory social insurance in case of temporary disability and in connection with motherhood" states that "Foreign citizens and stateless persons temporarily staying in the Russian Federation (with the exception of highly qualified specialists in accordance with the Federal Law of July 25, 2002 N115-FZ On the Legal Status of Foreign Citizens in the Russian Federation") are entitled to receive insurance coverage in the form of temporary disability benefits, subject to the payment of insurance premiums for them by insurers, for a period of at least six months preceding the month in which the insured event occurred. Payment of sick leave to citizens of the Eurasian Economic Union (EAEU) working under labor contracts, including highly qualified specialists temporarily staying in the Russian Federation, is no different from paying sick leave to Russian citizens (part 2, article 1.1 of Law N 255-FZ, paragraph 3, article 98 section XXVI of the Treaty on the Eurasian Economic Union (EAEU), Letter of the Ministry of Labor of Russia dated November18, 2015 N 17-3/B-560).

### q113 · Right of temporary migrants to become lawfully settled.
- Law: Yes, there is an opportunity to switch to permanent status after certain conditions are met
- Implementation: Usually

National law in the Russian Federation protects this right in Article 2.1, Article 6, Article 6.1, and Article 8 of the Federal Law dated July 25, 2002 No. 115-FZ “On the Legal Status of Foreign Citizens in the Russian Federation." The status of permanent residence is assigned only from the moment of obtaining a residence permit. A temporary residence permit does not grant the migrant the status of permanent residence, but nevertheless gives the right to stay in the Russian Federation for a long time (three years), without obtaining additional documents.
