Before you publish
  1. Arrests are not people.The counts are of arrests. One person can be arrested more than once. Likely duplicate records are removed.
  2. Interior is not border."Interior arrests" are ICE arrests away from the border. Border Patrol apprehensions are a separate dataset (CBP).
  3. A city can be a jail.Conroe, Florence and Sierra Blanca rank high because of custody transfers recorded there. Say "recorded in", not "residents of".
  4. 287(g) arrests are an undercount.Some custodial arrests that came through 287(g) programs are coded as custodial.
  5. Sex is officer-observed since about April 2025.It is no longer self-reported gender. Comparisons across that date need a note.
  6. Convictions are ICE's record.Convictions in other countries are not included.
  7. Some deportation data doesn't add up.We flag where it disagrees with government reports, and the Atlas doesn't use those tables. Read the note
