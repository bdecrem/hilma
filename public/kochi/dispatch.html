§ I  ·  What it is

Kochi is a chat application. Most of the time, you talk to it the way you talk to anything else — you type, it answers, the screen is quiet.

It does not read your calendar. It does not summarize your inbox. It would not want to.

§ II  ·  The signal

Once a day, at an hour we do not announce, your phone buzzes. The agent has said something. You did not ask for it.

The instruction is signed before it leaves us, and verified before it speaks. We chose the key. We did not choose the hour.
