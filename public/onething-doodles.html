On Onething you keep one sentence a day. Opus 5.5, on low effort, reads it and draws one small doodle in the margin, the way someone with a pen would in twenty seconds: one image, a few confident strokes, one red accent, a little wit.

Sept 3Woke up with the answer to the thing that stumped me all week, before the sun was even up.
Sept 7Walked the cliffs with the dog, then came home to find the kids had built a castle out of every cushion in the house.
Sept 11Finally untangled the dependency mess that has been eating the afternoons.

These three are from Bart's own September page. They are also the examples in the prompt, so every new doodle is drawn in this hand. The recipe: docs/opus55/onething-doodles.md.
