An AI agent for activists and academics

Hi. This is something I’m building for a few friends, community groups and researchers.

Most of you don’t love Big Tech, and you think hard about where your data goes. So I’ve set up a system on one computer in my office, just for this. It can build code, websites and data analysis. You decide what data you give it, and it runs on open models, so none of it goes to an AI company.

It isn’t bulletproof. It’s one machine and one person, me. What I can promise is that you’ll always know where your stuff is and what’s happening to it, and you can see all of it and delete it anytime.

I want to find out whether AI can be small, honest, and actually useful to people doing work that matters. Starting in December, with about 10 of you.
