An AI agent for activists and academics

Hi. This is something I’m building for friends, community groups and researchers.

Most of you don’t love Big Tech, and you think hard about where your data goes. So I’ve built an AI agent that runs on its own machine, just for this. It builds code, websites and data analysis. You decide what data you give it, and it runs on open models, so none of it goes to an AI company.

You’ll always know where your stuff is and what’s happening to it, and you can see all of it and delete it anytime. Here’s how it works.

We’re starting small, but we think the idea is pretty big: AI that works for the people doing work that matters, and answers to them. The first group starts in December.
