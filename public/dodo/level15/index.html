Peck · Jelly Lagoon · Level 15

Two games for the rest stop

Level 15 sits in the middle of the lagoon, so both payoffs are on the water at dusk. Pick one. Each is a self-contained HTML mockup with sound, a start card, a score and a best; phone first, in a phone frame on a desktop.

A · RHYTHM

Pebble Skip

Drag back and let go to throw a pebble across the lagoon, then tap in rhythm each time it is about to touch the water — every skip lights a floating lantern, and the window shrinks as the run grows. Five pebbles, the dodo cheering from the shore.

Play Pebble Skip
B · DIVE

Deep Dive

The dodo in a bubble helmet sinks while you hold and floats when you let go: collect pearls, slip past gummy jellyfish, and breathe at bubble pockets as the water turns dark and bioluminescent. Score is depth in metres plus pearls.

Play Deep Dive
